The drink

An Aperol Spritz as it is served. Three liquids, ice, a slice of orange. Take it apart and the question is which part is doing the work.

By volume

Half of it is Prosecco. A third is Aperol. The rest is soda. That is what the recipe says, and it is the least useful thing you can know about the drink.

  • Prosecco50%
  • Aperol33%
  • Sparkling water17%

By impact

Weigh each part by the work it does instead, and it inverts. Prosecco is half the glass and a quarter of the drink. Aperol is a third of the glass and more than half of it.

What it does to you

Even that only tells you what is in the glass. What the drink actually does to you is a different measurement: five senses, eleven effects, three groups. None of it is visible in the pour.
